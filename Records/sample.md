### Description
This is an **interactive problem**. 
Noora ordered $k$ different dishes out of $n$ available menu items ($1 \le k \le n \le 10^5$). 
Leha must guess the numbers of any two dishes ordered by Noora using at most 60 queries.

In each query, Leha specifies two dish numbers $(x, y)$. Noora answers:
- **`TAK`** if $\text{dist}(x) \le \text{dist}(y)$
- **`NIE`** otherwise

where $\text{dist}(p)$ is the absolute distance from dish $p$ to the nearest dish ordered by Noora.
